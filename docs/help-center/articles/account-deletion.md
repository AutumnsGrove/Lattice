# Deleting Your Account

If you decide to leave Grove, you can request deletion of your account and all associated data. Here's how it works.

## Two ways an account goes away

There are two different paths, and they feel different on purpose.

- **You are leaving.** You asked us to delete your account, or you asked support to. You get time, an export, and a human on the other end of the email. That's most of this article.
- **We removed your account.** We found a violation of the [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use) — usually spam or a link farm — and took the account down. That has its own section at the end: [If we removed your account](#if-we-removed-your-account).

If you're not sure which one happened, check your email. A removal always sends one.

## Before you request deletion

**Export your content first.** Once your account is deleted, we can't recover it. If there's anything you want to keep, export it before proceeding.

Go to **Account & Subscription** in your admin panel and scroll to the **Your Data** section. You can download all your posts, pages, and media information.

See [Exporting Your Content](/knowledge/help/exporting-your-content) for details on what's included.

## How to delete your account

Account deletion is handled through support to ensure you don't accidentally lose your data.

1. Go to **Account & Subscription** in your admin panel
2. Scroll to the **Danger Zone** section
3. Click the email link to contact us
4. Let us know you'd like to delete your account
5. We'll confirm you've exported anything you want to keep
6. We process the deletion within 30 days

We'll help you through the process and make sure you have everything you need before we remove anything.

## What gets deleted

**Immediately:**
- Your admin panel access, and every active session
- Your blog becomes inaccessible
- Your posts are unpublished
- Your images and uploads are removed from storage
- Your subdomain is released
- Your identity is deleted from our sign-in system

**Within 24 hours:**
- Your account data is removed from active systems

**Within 90 days:**
- All data is permanently purged from backups
- Nothing recoverable remains

We keep one thing: a record that the removal happened — when, why, and how much of each kind of row was removed. It has none of your content in it. It's how we can answer "what happened to this account?" later, including for ourselves.

## If we removed your account

Sometimes we remove an account ourselves rather than waiting to be asked. This
happens when an account breaks the [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use)
— most often because it was created to place outbound links, publish doorway
content for another site, or pump out template posts at scale.

**You will get an email.** It goes to the address on the account, and it says
what we found, which rule it broke, and what was removed. If we removed your
account this way, the email is the first you'll hear of it — we act first and
write immediately after, because telling a spam account it is about to be removed
mostly gives it time to publish more.

**What's gone:** everything. The same list as above — access, content, uploads,
the subdomain, the sign-in identity. Backups clear on the usual 90-day clock.

**What isn't offered:** an export. We don't hand back the content we removed for
abuse. If you believe we got it wrong — a shared network, a false positive, a
site you didn't know was a link farm — reply to the email and say so. We read
those, and we reverse removals when we're wrong.

**Coming back:** an account removed for abuse isn't eligible for a new account
for one year, and creating one under a different email alias doesn't reset that
clock. If you'd like to come back sooner, ask. The answer is sometimes yes.

## What about my custom domain?

If you're on Oak or Evergreen with a custom domain:

**Domain you brought (BYOD):** The domain is yours. Remove the DNS records pointing to Grove, and the domain is free to use elsewhere.

**Domain included with Evergreen:** The domain is registered in your name. Request a transfer authorization code before deleting your account. We'll provide it within 48 hours. Once transferred to another registrar, the domain is fully yours.

## Data retention timeline

| Data Type | When Deleted |
|-----------|--------------|
| Blog posts | Immediately unpublished, purged within 24 hours |
| Images/media | Removed from storage with the account |
| Account info | Removed within 24 hours |
| Comments on your posts | Deleted with your posts |
| Your comments on other blogs | Anonymized, not deleted |
| Backup copies | Purged within 90 days |

## Cancellation vs. deletion

These are different things:

**Cancellation:** Stops your subscription. Your account remains, data stays, you just can't access paid features. Good if you might come back.

**Deletion:** Removes everything. Your account, data, and blog are gone permanently. Only do this if you're sure.

If you just want to stop paying, cancel your subscription instead. Your blog stays live on whatever plan you qualify for (or becomes inaccessible if you have no free tier equivalent).

## If you change your mind

**Before deletion completes:** Since we process deletions manually, you can contact us to cancel the request any time before we've started. Just reply to your original email.

**After deletion completes:** We cannot recover your account. The data is gone. You'd need to create a new account and start fresh.

## Legal requests

Under GDPR and similar laws, you have the right to request deletion of your personal data. Deleting your account fulfills this right. If you have specific questions about data deletion for legal purposes, contact us directly.

## A final note

We're sorry to see you go, but we respect your choice. Your data has always been yours—deleting it is your right.

If something about Grove drove you away, we'd genuinely appreciate knowing what. A quick note to support helps us improve. But no pressure—you can delete silently if you prefer.
