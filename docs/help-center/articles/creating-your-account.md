# Creating Your Account

Getting started with Grove takes about a minute. Here's what to expect.

## How sign-up works

Grove has no passwords. You choose between two ways in: your Google account, or a six-digit code we email you. Signing up and signing in are the same screen, so there's nothing separate to figure out.

![The Plant onboarding flow — creating your Grove account in minutes](https://cdn.grove.place/docs/help/plant-onboarding.png)

### Signing up with Google

1. Go to [grove.place](https://grove.place) and click **Get Started**
2. Click **Continue with Google**
3. Select your Google account (or sign in to Google if you aren't already)
4. You're in

### Signing up with email

Not a Google person, or just don't want to use it here? Email works the same way.

1. Go to [grove.place](https://grove.place) and click **Get Started**
2. Enter your email address and click **Continue with email**
3. Check your inbox for a message titled "*(your code)* is your Grove sign-in code"
4. Type the six-digit code into the page you came from and click **Sign in**
5. You're in

New here? That's all it takes. There's no separate sign-up form, and an email address we haven't seen before becomes a new account once the code is entered.

A few things worth knowing about codes:

- They expire after 10 minutes and work once.
- Three wrong guesses and the code is used up. Request a fresh one with **Send a new code**.
- You can request up to three codes every 10 minutes. This keeps strangers from filling someone's inbox.
- Pasting with a space or dash in the middle (like `123 456`) is fine.
- Made a typo in your address? Click **Use a different email** to start over.

> 💡 **Tip:** If the email doesn't show up within a minute or two, check your spam or junk folder. It comes from `auth@grove.place`.

## Why no passwords?

**No passwords to forget.** Whether you use Google or an emailed code, there's nothing to remember and nothing to reuse across sites.

**Better security.** Passwords get leaked, guessed, and recycled. A code that lives for ten minutes and works once leaves much less to steal. If you use Google, its two-factor authentication protects you too.

**Faster setup.** No "create a password, confirm the password, check your email to verify" dance. You're signed in as soon as you've proven who you are.

## What Grove gets when you sign in

**With Google**, Google shares:

- Your email address
- Your name (as it appears on your Google account)
- Your profile picture (if you have one)

Grove only receives the minimum information needed to identify you. We can't see your emails, files, contacts, or anything else.

**With email**, Grove has your email address and nothing else. We don't ask for a name or a picture.

## Choosing your blog address

After signing in for the first time, you'll choose your blog's URL:

```
yourusername.grove.place
```

Pick something you're happy with—this becomes your address on the web. Keep it short, memorable, and easy to type.

> **Note:** You can't change your blog address after creating it. Choose carefully.

## What happens next

Once your account is created:

- Your blog is live immediately, though empty
- You can access your admin panel at `yourblog.grove.place/admin`
- An optional tour walks you through the basics

From there, you can customize your blog's appearance, write your first post, or explore the settings. There's no rush.

## Signing in later

Coming back? Same process:

1. Go to your blog's admin URL or grove.place
2. Click **Sign In**
3. Continue with Google, or enter your email and type the code we send

Use the same method you signed up with, or the same email address. Grove recognizes you by your email, so a Google account and an emailed code for the same address open the same blog.

Grove remembers you for about 30 days. After that, you'll need to sign in again.
