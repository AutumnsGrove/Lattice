# What are Rings?

Count the rings of a tree and you learn its story.

Rings will be Grove's analytics system—private insights for writers, not public metrics for vanity. You'll be able to see how your posts perform, understand what resonates, track your growth over time. But never in real-time. Never compared to others. Never designed to make you anxious.

> **Not built yet.** Rings is designed but does not exist. There is no analytics dashboard to open today, and nothing on this page is something you can use right now. What follows is why we want to build it, not a description of what you will find when you sign in. [Grove Workshop](/workshop#tool-rings) tracks where it sits.

## Why Rings will exist

Traditional analytics are designed for marketers. Real-time dashboards. Constant refresh-checking. "Your views are down 20% this week!" Notifications that pull you back when you should be writing.

That isn't what writers need. Writers need to know if their work is finding readers. They need to understand what resonates and what doesn't. They need motivation to keep going, not anxiety about metrics they can't control.

Rings would take a wellness-first approach. All stats delayed 24 hours—no dopamine hits from watching numbers climb. Nothing comparing you to other writers. Silence treated as neutral, not failure. The goal is insight without obsession.

## How it would work

Rings is meant to show several kinds of information:

**Aggregate stats** — Page views, unique readers, popular posts. Big-picture understanding of your blog's reach.

**Resonance indicators** — Instead of vote counts, signals like "Sparked Interest" (above your average) or "Really Resonated" (your top 25%). These would compare you only to yourself.

**Reading patterns** — Which posts get read all the way through? Which get abandoned halfway? This is meant to tell you what truly connects.

**Geographic overview** — Where in the world are your readers? (No individual tracking—just country-level aggregates.)

All of it private. No public view counts. No leaderboards. No one seeing your numbers but you.

### Delayed by design

Rings would not show real-time data. Everything delayed 24 hours. That's the point:

- No refresh-checking compulsion
- Focus on writing, not watching
- Data reflecting settled patterns, not momentary spikes

### Focus periods

The design includes Focus Periods: hiding your stats for a week, a month, or indefinitely. When you're deep in a project, metrics can wait.

**Note:** Rings is not yet in development. When it does land, it will be available to Sapling tier and above.

## What this will mean for you

**Insight without anxiety.** Learning what works without developing a metrics addiction.

**Privacy by default.** Your stats yours alone. No public displays.

**Wellness first.** Every feature designed around the question: "Does this help writers, or does this exploit them?"

**Your baseline, not theirs.** Resonance indicators comparing you to your own history, never to other writers.
